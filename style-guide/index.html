Imagery

Image style

All game and promo art follows one look: glossy, high-contrast 3D illustration lit by neon. Every image uses the same recipe. Each point below can be seen in the approved examples.

Always

  • One clear subject: a character, a dealer, or a single symbol (book, 7, clover, diamond).
  • Neon rim light in the card's accent colour. Light trails or sparks curl around the subject.
  • Deep background: navy, black-violet, or one saturated hue for New Games.
  • Rich, saturated colour with crisp highlights on gold, gems and coins.
  • Friendly, confident characters looking at the viewer, from the chest up.
  • Game logo in the lower third (tiles) or upper right (character cards).

Never

  • Photographs, flat vector art or muted colour.
  • Buttons, prices, coin amounts, RTP numbers or player counts inside the image.
  • A border or glow drawn into the image. Borders are always CSS.
  • Imitations of a real provider's game art. Licensed titles use the provider's own approved assets.
  • Cash, cards or chips shown as real-money prizes.
  • Stretching. Crop to the ratio instead.

CMS & editable content

CMS

How content is edited

Request to the development team. We ask that the website be built as a modular, CMS-driven platform. Our goal is to add and edit as much content as possible ourselves, without commissioning a new page or developer work each time.

Specifically, we request that:

  • Every page (Home, Promotions, Guides, game pages and any future page) is assembled from reusable blocks that our team can add, remove, reorder and fill in.
  • We can create new pages (landing pages, campaigns, seasonal pages) from these existing blocks, with no new build needed.
  • Text, images, links, banner sizes and which devices a block shows on are editable by our team.
  • The visual design of each block stays locked to this style guide, so every page remains on-brand whoever edits it.

The sections below describe the editing capabilities we would like the CMS to support.

Global contentHeader (logo, button label, news ticker), main menu labels and order, footer links, newsletter text, legal bar. Edited once, used on every page.
Page contentThe blocks on each page: banners, promo cards, game rows, guide lists, FAQ, text. Each page has its own list.
CollectionsPromotions, Guides (articles, categories, videos), FAQs and ticker items are lists that blocks can pull from automatically.

  • Content Manager

Staff accounts with the Editor or Admin role see a Content Manager item at the bottom of the main menu, under My Profile, with a layout-template icon. Players never see it. How Sweet Bonanza works

Sweet Bonanza drops traditional paylines completely. The grid is 6×5, and a win needs 8 or more identical symbols to land anywhere on it, regardless of position. This is a Cluster Pays system, which gives every spin a wider net of possible hits than classic payline slots.

When a winning combination forms, the Tumble feature (cascading reels) kicks in. Winning symbols disappear and new ones fall to fill the empty spaces. This repeats until no more wins form, which in theory can give an unlimited number of cascades from a single spin. There is no wild symbol in the game. The scatter, a lollipop, pays instantly for 4, 5 or 6 symbols (3×, 5× and 100× the stake) and triggers the free spins.

RTP, volatility and win potential

Sweet Bonanza's RTP is set at 96.51%, above the industry average of roughly 95–96%. In practice the game theoretically pays back €96.51 for every €100 wagered, calculated over a very large number of spins. Worth knowing: Pragmatic Play offers several RTP configurations, and different sites can run the game at lower settings than the default. Always check the RTP value your site uses.

Volatility is rated 4 out of 5, which is above average. Sessions without meaningful payouts can be long, followed by sudden big hits. The max win is 21,100× the stake, which at the maximum bet of €100 equals €2,110,000. In practice that potential is concentrated in the bonus round, where stacked multipliers drive the really big wins. The Ante Bet raises the stake by 25% and doubles the chance of triggering scatters, effectively lowering volatility to medium-high, so players can adjust how the game plays.

Theme and presentation

Candy shop meets dreamland. The grid is framed by striped candy canes, the background is filled with cotton-candy clouds and trees shaped like ice-cream cones. The symbols (apples, bananas, grapes, plums, watermelons and colourful sweets) are clear and easy to read, even during fast cascades. The red heart-shaped sweet is the most valuable symbol and pays up to 50× the stake for 12 matching symbols.

The graphics are high quality on every platform. The game is built in HTML5 and runs without performance issues on desktop, tablet and phone. The soundtrack is lively and sugary, creating a carnival feel that fits the theme without becoming intrusive. Some players find the intense colour palette overwhelming in long sessions, but for most it adds to the game's instant recognisability.

Free spins with multiplier bombs up to 100×

Four or more scatter symbols (lollipops) trigger 10 free spins. The number of scatters also sets an instant payout: 4 scatters pay 3× the stake, 5 pay 5× and 6 pay 100×, on top of the free spins. During the free spins, colourful bomb symbols can land with random multiplier values between 2× and 100×. The Tumble feature stays active, and when a winning sequence ends, all multiplier values that landed during it are added together and the total multiplies the whole win. The free spins cannot be retriggered, but the bonus round still holds the game's full win potential of 21,100×. The chance of the really big hits is low, but the mechanic is transparent and easy to follow.

The Bonus Buy lets players jump straight to the free spins for 100× the current stake, cutting out the wait in the base game and giving instant access to the multiplier bombs. It is not available in every market, and availability can vary by site and local rules. For players who want more scatters without buying the bonus outright, the Ante Bet (a 25% stake increase) works as a middle ground.

Bet range and bankroll

Bets range from €0.20 to €100 per spin, covering everyone from careful players with a small bankroll to high rollers. Autoplay and quickspin are available for players who prefer a faster pace.

With volatility at 4 out of 5, patience is needed. Long dry spells without big payouts are normal, and the game suits players who can ride out swings over many spins. Keeping individual bets at the lower end of the range gives more spins per session and lowers the risk of the bankroll running out before the bonus round triggers. The Ante Bet raises the stake by 25% but doubles the scatter frequency: a choice for players who put bonus frequency ahead of the lowest cost per spin.
