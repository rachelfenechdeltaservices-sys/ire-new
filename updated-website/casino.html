How We Rate

How we score online casinos in Ireland

Each casino goes through the same detailed process. Our specialists create real accounts, make real deposits, play real-money sessions across slots and live tables, test withdrawals and contact support. No brand can pay to be listed or to improve its score.

Licensing & compliance

Every casino we list must hold a licence from a recognised authority such as the GRAI, MGA or UKGC, with robust age verification and AML procedures in place. Any brand we can't verify is left out — no exceptions.

Bonus value & wagering

We look past the headline number to the playthrough multiplier, game weightings, maximum bet rules, free-spin values and expiry windows, then judge how realistic the offer is to clear. Transparent, low-wagering deals rank highest.

Game library & providers

We count the titles, check the range — slots, jackpots, live dealer, table games and game shows — and look at who supplies them. Studios like Evolution, Pragmatic Play and NetEnt are a strong sign of quality, and casinos that publish RTP figures earn extra credit.

Payments & withdrawals

We fund real accounts and cash out, recording payment options, fees and actual payout times. Casinos that release funds within 24 hours outscore those with long pending periods.

Mobile & live streaming

We test the apps and mobile site for loading speed, stability and how well live-dealer streams perform on a phone. If the casino only really works on desktop, it won't score well with us.

Player protection

Deposit limits, session reminders, reality checks and self-exclusion should be easy to set up. We reward casinos that treat safer gambling as a core feature rather than a box-ticking exercise.
